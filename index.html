Long stories become
difficult to remember.

A detail in chapter seven can change everything in chapter seventy.

As a universe grows, so does everything an author needs to hold together. Notes, documents and spreadsheets can become increasingly difficult to search and maintain.

  • Character histories & relationships
  • Locations & chronology
  • Factions & political structures
  • Technology & world rules
  • Previous promises & foreshadowing
  • Historical events & old details

Half Story is being designed around modern large language models, with Claude being evaluated for long-context story analysis, structured information extraction, semantic retrieval, continuity checking and author-assistance workflows.

Every world starts
with a story.

Built from a real
long-form storytelling problem.

Half Story began as an attempt to manage the growing complexity of an original long-form web novel. As characters, locations, technologies, political systems, timelines and historical events accumulated, maintaining a consistent internal canon became increasingly difficult.

The project explores how AI, semantic retrieval and structured knowledge systems can help long-form authors understand and manage their fictional universes.

Student-built in Romania.

Half Story is an early-stage independent software project created by Adelin Toader, a Computer Engineering student in Romania. The project is currently in concept and prototype development.
